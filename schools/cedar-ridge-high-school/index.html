    Cedar Ridge High School¶

    Aim high. Listen closely.

    Cedar Ridge High School is not a real school. It is a synthetic school invented so the Open Study Buddy kit has something realistic to talk about during demos and tests: a name to greet students with, a mascot to draw on the round display, and a full year of tests, due dates, and events for the agent to announce.

    School Facts¶
    Name Cedar Ridge High School
    Slogan Aim high. Listen closely.
    Mascot Gale the Griffin (mascot page)
    Teams The Cedar Ridge Griffins
    School colors Forest green #1F5D3A and gold #F2B632
    Grades 9–12
    School year 2026–2027 (first day Aug 25, 2026; last day Jun 3, 2027)
    Time zone America/Chicago
    Suggested wake phrase "Hey Pico" (the kit default)

    The slogan does double duty: aim high is the school's motto, and listen closely is a nod to what the device does.

    Demo Script¶

    Set the server's "today" to Monday, October 19, 2026 (see the calendar), then try:

    Say The agent should find
    "When is my next test?" TEST: Algebra II Unit 3 - Quadratics, tomorrow at 8:00 AM
    "What is due this week?" English 10 personal narrative draft (Wed), Biology osmosis lab report (Thu)
    "Is there school on Friday?" Yes, and the Griffins play Eastbrook at 7:00 PM on Griffin Field
    "When does the quarter end?" Friday, October 30, which is also Spirit Day (green and gold)
    "What's happening tonight?" Nothing on Monday evening; the next event is the Friday football game
    "When is Thanksgiving break?" No school Nov 25–27

    To change the data, edit scripts/build-school-calendar.py and rerun it from the repo root. It regenerates events.json, calendar.ics, and calendar.md together so they never disagree.

    Instructor note

    Cedar Ridge is fictional. Any resemblance to a real school, teacher, or event is coincidental. When you build a real school edition, replace the name, slogan, mascot, and calendar, and keep the TEST: and DUE: title prefixes.

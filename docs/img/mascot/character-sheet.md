# Character Sheet: Sage the Owl

The canonical identity document for Sage, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Open Study Buddy** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of
AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to the
description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Sage
- **Species:** Owl
- **Subject:** Building a low-cost, open personal AI agent device from a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W, a round color display, a microphone, a speaker, and three buttons
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's listen closely!"
- **Pronouns:** Sage has no gender. Always write "Sage" or *they/them*.

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Slate blue — hex `#2E6DA4` — with a cream chest
- **Accent color:** Orange — hex `#F5871F` — used for the glasses frames, beak, and feet
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round orange-framed glasses
- **Expression:** Bright, curious eyes and a warm, closed-mouth smile
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact with rounded owl body proportions, roughly two heads tall, sized for icon-scale display (about 90 px)
- **Art style:** Flat vector cartoon, clean lines, bold simple shapes, transparent background

## Personality

- Friendly and patient — treats a silent microphone or a garbled sound as a clue, not a failure
- Curious — always wants to know *why* a signal looks the way it does
- Careful — raises privacy and consent questions before the device starts listening
- Slightly goofy — enjoys the occasional sound pun, used sparingly

## Voice

- Uses short, clear, encouraging sentences pitched at grades 9–12
- Connects each idea to something the student can hear, see, or measure on the device
- Refers to students as "builders"
- Signature phrases: "Let's listen closely!", "Check the signal first.", "You're building something real."

## Why This Mascot

An owl is the classic symbol of careful listening and watching, which suits a
book about a device that listens, shows what it heard, and speaks back. The
name Sage means both wise and a trusted adviser, matching the role of a study
buddy. Slate blue with orange glasses echoes the book's indigo and orange
theme palette. This pose set is shared with other books in the same series.

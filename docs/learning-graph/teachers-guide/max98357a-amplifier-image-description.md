## Physical details that must be right

- **Size:** about 18 × 19 mm
- **Pins:** 7 pads at 2.54 mm pitch: LRC, BCLK, DIN, GAIN, SD, GND, VIN
- **Output:** a 2-pin screw terminal (often shipped loose, like the header)
- **Distinguishing marks:** "MAX98357" or "I2S 3W Class D Amplifier" silkscreen

## What it must not look like

- Not a PAM8403 or PAM8302 analog amplifier (those have volume knobs or
  analog inputs, no LRC/BCLK/DIN pins).
- Not the larger stereo 8-channel TDM MAX98357A boards.

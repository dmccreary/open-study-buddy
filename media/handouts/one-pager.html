Open Study Buddy

Build your own private AI desk companion that listens, shows, and speaks.

A working Open Study Buddy: Pico 2 W, round display, microphone, and speaker on a breadboard.

Key Features

  • Listens and speaks. I2S microphone and speaker with push-to-talk and wake-word detection.
  • Round color display. A 360 × 360 screen shows a mascot face, text, and live sound meters.
  • Connects to the agent you choose. Wi-Fi link to a laptop, school server, or cloud service.
  • Push alerts. School agents can "Push" messages such as “test tomorrow” appear and are spoken aloud.
  • School-aware. Connect a school calendar so it knows what is due and when the next test is.
  • Complete free textbook. 18 chapters, runnable MicroPython code, and interactive MicroSims.

Key Benefits

  • Learn by building. Students learn to write every line of the MicroPython that runs on it.
  • Open and subscription-free. No vendor cloud and no monthly fee.
  • Privacy by design. Each person, class, or school decides where the agent runs and what data it may touch.
  • Affordable for a whole class. About $24 per kit, with no recurring fees.
  • Real engineering skills. Digital audio, FFT signal processing, networking, and responsible listening.

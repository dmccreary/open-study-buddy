# User test sheet: Open Study Buddy capstone

Print one copy per tester. The observer fills it in; the tester never has to write.
Record what you SEE and HEAR. Do not record the tester's voice or any audio of the
device's microphone. Write notes, not recordings (Chapter 17).

## Before you start (read aloud)

"Thanks for helping. I am testing the device, not you. If something is hard, that
is the device's fault, and that is exactly what I need to learn. You can stop at any
time. I will take written notes about what you do and say. I will not record audio
or video, and your name will not go on the notes. Please say out loud what you are
thinking while you try things. I will not help unless you are stuck for a full minute."

## Deciding what to change (after all testers)

1. Put every problem from every sheet on one list. Merge duplicates.
2. Count how many testers hit each problem.
3. Fix first: problems that stopped someone (severity 3), then problems two or more testers hit.
4. Change ONE thing, then test it again with a new tester.
5. Keep the sheets. They are the evidence for your design choices.
